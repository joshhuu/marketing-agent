## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T USE:
- "Sales Director" → Use `Director of Sales`
- "Marketing Director" → Use `Director of Marketing`
- "Head of IT" → Use `Director of IT`
- "Engineering Manager" → Use `VP of Engineering` or `IT Manager`
- "Chief Security Officer" → Use `CISO`
- "Tech Lead" → Use `Software Engineer` or `CTO`

### ✅ DO USE:
- Exact titles from the reference list above
- Multiple titles in one prompt for broader reach
- Specific product benefits in your messaging
- Quantifiable metrics (percentages, time saved, etc.)

## 💡 Pro Tips for Presentations

1. **Start with a C-Suite prompt** (CEO, CFO, CTO) - These often have fewer prospects and execute faster
2. **Use multi-role prompts** for variety - Shows the system handling different personas
3. **Include specific metrics** - Makes the generated content more compelling
4. **Approve 5-10 prospects** - Shows the human-in-the-loop workflow
5. **Check prompt logs after execution** - Demonstrates transparency and compliance
